F1.1 Where a parity doublet changes the reading (DERIVED, then MEASURED).
At y = 0 the sectors +P and -P are exactly degenerate, so every lowest level with P != 0 is an exact doublet: even (|+P> + |-P>)/sqrt2 and odd. B lowers P by 2, so B connects the two members only at P = +-1.
 - |P| >= 2 (the plane waves at N >= 3): <B> = 0 on both members at y = 0. Nothing changes.
 - |P| = 1 (odd N only, see F3): <B>_even = +(1/2) sum_j (j+1) c_j^2 > 0, <B>_odd = minus that. So the member held is a reading choice there. The N = 3 row is this case.
At y != 0 the even-mix state is (psi_+ + psi_-)/sqrt2 with psi_+ the perturbed plane wave and psi_- its mirror. Exchange sends B to B^dag and A to A^dag, so <psi_-|B|psi_-> = <psi_+|B|psi_+> and the same for A. The cross terms <psi_+|B|psi_-> need the charge to travel from +N to -N, order y^(2N-2). So B's sign and size in the even mix come from ONE plane wave. The mix contributes only the exponentially small splitting.
Measured even against odd (out/t1_parity.hbp, own block solver at K bits, no engine code): on all the below-edge points, N = 3, 9, 27 at every s and N = 81 at the rows it reached, the even level is the lower one, on every row. F4.2 The coefficient from theory (DERIVED here by hand, then MEASURED against F4.1).
The chain at charge P in the gauge (-1)^j has an exact Weyl symbol in tau = (j + P/2)/N and p conjugate to j:

U = a+^dag a0 + a0^dag a- has the exact symbol sqrt(N sigma + 1/2) [ sqrt(N tau + 1/2 + Q/2) e^(-ip/2) - sqrt(N tau + 1/2 - Q/2) e^(ip/2) ], Q = P + 1/2 between chains P and P + 1. B from P = 2 to 0 is -sqrt((N tau + 1/2)^2 - 1/4), diagonal in tau, and from P = 1 to -1 it is +(N tau + 1/2).
Expand about tau = r = rho, p = 0, with hbar = 1/N. With z = rho0, a = 4 - 3u, omega^2 = 4 a r z, c0 = (z/r - u)/4:
   <tau> = r + t1/N,  t1 = -1/(2a) - (1 - 4r)/(2 omega).
