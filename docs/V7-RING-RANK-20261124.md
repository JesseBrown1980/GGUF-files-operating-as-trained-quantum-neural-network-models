## Constructive upper bound
Let y=P x and let L be inverse first-layer pair SUM composed with P^-1. In forward coordinates before dressing rotations, the amplitude is product over q of local integer vectors v_q((L y)_q). Split coordinates into left and right. Each mixed row of L is assigned to X or Y. Record alpha = values of the left restrictions of X and beta = values of the right restrictions of Y. For each pair of attainable signatures, define left vector u as indicator of alpha times the pure-left factors and the Y factors evaluated with beta. Define right vector v symmetrically with beta, pure-right and X factors with alpha. Sum u tensor v over signatures; entrywise identity holds because exactly one signature pair is selected by each input address. Local dressing rotations act separately on u and v in forward coordinates and preserve rank (all numerator rotation determinants nonzero). The final inverse ring is an address permutation, restored for each query.
