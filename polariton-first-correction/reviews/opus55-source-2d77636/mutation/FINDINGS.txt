F9. THE CERTIFICATE'S LOGIC, READ ALONG THE WAY. DERIVED, from src/bin/polariton_first_correction.rs at head, by hand, as far as a mutation lane needs it. A separate lane owns the review of the derivation; this is what I read while choosing mutants, and I found no fault in it.
- Duhamel: psi_full(t) - psi_P(t) = -i Int_0^t exp(-iH(t-s)) Q H P psi_P(s) ds. B = QHP acts on shells K-1 (pair) and K (pair, a, b) only: b_norm's closure and even_up agree, and K-2 cannot leave the block.
- In a window, psi_P(s) = computed + e with norm(e) <= eta = eps_round + eps_poly so far. computed = high + low at shell K-R, R = 2 degree + 1. B T_m(H~) low = 0 for m < degree since low + 2m stays at or under K-3. |J_m(z)| <= (z/2)^m/m!, monotone in z, covers tau in [0,h]. So norm(B psi_P(s+tau)) <= norm(B)(b + rho + eta), which is what `bound` adds, floor corrected by +1.
- The recurrence's rounding: e_{k+1} = 2 H~ e_k - e_{k-1} + d_k gives e_k = Sum U_{k-1-j}(H~) d_j, norm(U_n) <= n+1, norm(d_j) <= sqrt(dim) units, so k(k+1)/2 sqrt(dim): the weight's factor. The elements' error: z * norm(dH~) <= z * 8 units by the row sum. Table entries: 4 units a coefficient, the route's limit, doubled and doubled for Re and Im.
- The observables: with e = psi_full - computed, norm(e) <= eps, <O>_full - <O>_computed = 2 Re<computed|O|e> + <e|O|e>, so eps(2 norm(O computed) + norm(O) eps). x_up and s_up are read on the computed state over the full space (the outgoing images are in xu, sa), as that form asks. norm(X) <= (N+1)^2 + N and norm(S) = sqrt(2) N <= 2(N+1) both hold.
- Reads at the block: <P>, <A>, <B> sum only over pairs inside the block, and the computed state lives in the block, so nothing is dropped.
