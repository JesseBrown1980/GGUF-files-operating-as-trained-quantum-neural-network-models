Weyl (symmetric) rules: a^dag a -> |c|^2 - 1/2; distinct modes multiply; (a^dag a)^2 -> |c|^4 - |c|^2; a0 a0 -> c0^2; P -> conj(c+) conj(c-) c0^2.

F2.1 Why an ensemble can carry the whole first correction. Exact number-conserving map on the N-particle space (as in the second

First order: <O(t)> = <O_I(t)>_0 + (i/N) Int_0^t <[H1_I(tau), O_I(t)]>_0 dtau, interaction picture along H2.
  (i)  H2 is quadratic, so its flow maps Weyl symbols exactly: (U2^dag F U2)_W = F_W o Phi2.
  (ii) The Moyal bracket equals the Poisson bracket when one side is at most quadratic (the next term needs three derivatives on
       each side). O's leading part is linear (A/sqrt N -> z = b+ + conj b-) or quadratic (X/N -> |z|^2 - 1).
  (iii) O's 1/N-relative parts (the cubic part of A, the quartic part of X) need only <.>_0 along H2, exact by (i).

first order term by term. Weyl symbols needed, computed here:
  H1_W = -u[|b+|^4 + |b-|^4 + |b+|^2|b-|^2 - (3/2) n] - (n - 3/2)(conj b+ conj b- + b+ b-) + (s/2)(n - 3/2) Sum(b + conj b),
