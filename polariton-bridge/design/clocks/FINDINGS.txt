Definitions (NAMED, from the task): gamma^2 = 1 - delta^2, Yinf = 2 s^2/(1 + delta),
   t_q  = ln((1 + delta) N/(1 + Yinf))/(2 gamma),   t_tr = ln((1 + delta) N/Yinf)/(2 gamma),   t* = ln N/(2 gamma).
What they are, DERIVED from the law of PREDICTION.hbp line 39:
 - At large gamma t, 4 sinh^2(gamma t) -> e^(2 gamma t) and 8 (cosh(gamma t) - 1)^2 -> 2 e^(2 gamma t), so the law's
   C1 -> e^(2 gamma t)(1 + Yinf)/((1 + delta) N). t_q is the time this asymptote reaches 1: the law's C1, fluctuation plus
   coherent parts, reaches order one there and the law leaves its range. Its coherent part alone, A1^2/2 ->
   Yinf e^(2 gamma t)/((1 + delta) N), reaches 1 at t_tr: the scale at which one mean-field trajectory saturates.
   So t_tr > t_q always, and t_tr - t_q = ln((1 + Yinf)/Yinf)/(2 gamma).
 - At fixed s, t_tr - t_q does not depend on N. At fixed y, (1 + delta) N/Yinf = (1 + delta)^2/(2 y^2), so t_tr does not depend
   on N: t_tr = (5/8) ln(5832/25) = 3.407649655512683 at delta = 3/5 and (5/8) ln(729/50) = 1.674781704112820 at delta = -3/5,
   at all three N. The mean field in units JN = 1 does not know N at fixed y, and the clock says so.
 - At the blind points gamma = 4/5, 1/(2 gamma) = 5/8, and with N = 3^4, 3^6, 3^8, t* = (5/2) ln 3, (15/4) ln 3, 5 ln 3
   = 2.746530721670274, 4.119796082505411, 5.493061443340548. With D3 = ln(3)/(2 gamma) = (5/8) ln 3 = 0.686632680417568,
   t* = 4 D3, 6 D3, 8 D3 exactly: the ternary step divides t*.
 - t_q - t* = (5/8) ln((1 + delta)/(1 + Yinf)): at delta = -3/5 it is negative at every s; at delta = 3/5 it is positive only for
   Yinf < 3/5 (s = 1/3 and 2/3 here).
 - Zero field, y = 0: t_q = (5/8) ln((1 + delta) N); t_tr is undefined (Yinf = 0), and so is V^2.
